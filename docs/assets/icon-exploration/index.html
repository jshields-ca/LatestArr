A · Folded ribbon L

One ribbon folded into an L, with a bookmark notch at its end: the “L”, a saved item, and a fold that reads as paper or mail without drawing an envelope.

Assessment: Strong runner-up. Reads as an L at every size and is unlike anything nearby. The fold and notch are subtle at 32px and below, so refining it means a bolder crease and a deeper notch.

E · L with a “new” dot

A heavy L whose arm ends in a separate dot, the familiar “something new” badge. Monogram and meaning, with no extra symbol bolted on.

Assessment: Recommended. One bold glyph that stays crisp at 16px, reads as “L” plus “something new”, and isn't close to the *arr, Plex, Jellyfin, Tautulli, or mail-app icons. Refining it: weight balance, dot size and spacing, and corner radius matched to the UI.
